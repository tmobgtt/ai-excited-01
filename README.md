A goldfish the size of a city block hangs in the violet dusk, caught at the very top of a leap no fishbowl could ever contain. Its scales burn molten orange against the deep purple evening sky, and the water it carried with it falls away in droplets as large as cars, each one refracting the lights of the skyline below.

The city underneath is real and ordinary: office towers switching on their windows, streets settling into night. That contrast is the whole idea. One small creature, famous for living in the smallest possible world, suddenly occupies the largest one, and for a single suspended second it owns the sky.

This entry is a single still image, `entry.webp`, generated for the ai-excited-01 bounty on make.fun. No text, no props, no second subject: just scale, color, and the quiet shock of the impossible made calm.

## About the image

The fish is deliberately rendered with ordinary, believable anatomy: correct fin placement, a natural tail sweep, and scales that catch the low sun the way a real fish would. Nothing about the animal itself is exaggerated except its size, which is what makes the scene feel like a photograph of something that could never happen rather than a cartoon.

Composition keeps the goldfish as the single dominant subject, centered and lit warm against a cool violet backdrop, so the eye lands on it instantly. The falling droplets do the storytelling: their size quietly tells you how big the fish is, and their sparkle echoes the city lights far below, tying the creature to the world it is about to fall back into.
